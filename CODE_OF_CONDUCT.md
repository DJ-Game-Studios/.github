# Community standards

DJ Game Studios wants its public repositories to be useful, direct, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and technical disagreements respectfully.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Keep bug reports focused, reproducible, and free of private information.
- Respect project scope and maintainer decisions.
- Give credit for other people’s work and research.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted hostility.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone’s private information.
- Spam, repeated bad-faith demands, or deliberate disruption.
- Sharing credentials, exploit details, or sensitive logs in public threads.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should protect the community and the safety of the project.

If a concern includes a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.
