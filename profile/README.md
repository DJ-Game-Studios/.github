## Helix

**Helix is our main universe:** a fantasy-first world centered on evolution, identity, spirit, and transformation. Its first public game is **Helix 2000**, a 2D browser MMORPG in public alpha at [helix2000.com](https://helix2000.com). A larger persistent world is the long-term goal and is in private development.

## Our games

- **Helix 3D** — Our flagship 3D fantasy MMORPG, a persistent world in private development.
- **Helix 2000** — A browser MMORPG in public alpha with character classes, combat, quests, crafting, and social play. Play at [helix2000.com](https://helix2000.com).
- **DJ Engine / Helix Fracture** — Our own 2D engine and editor for story-driven games, with Helix Fracture as its first story. On hold while the studio focuses on Helix 3D and Helix 2000.
